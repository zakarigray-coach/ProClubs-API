# Weekly reporter voices

Confirmed articles alternate Raine and Teagan. Raine combines composed reporting (50%), thoughtful analysis (35%) and club warmth (15%). Teagan combines matchday energy (50%), light wit (35%) and a confident sports-desk voice (15%). Percentages describe editorial direction, not a mathematical text quota.

The factual draft retains management-selected matches and recorded statistics only. Current writing uses a curated voice-specific headline and introduction library. Each reporter's last eight openings are excluded, using the central SQLite `report_editorial` table. Regenerating a report retains its reporter and opening. A new report advances the rotation only when its draft transaction succeeds. Restarts do not reset the history. Essential factual wording can recur, and the library is finite; this does not promise unlimited unique prose.

Articles remain private review drafts. Optional player-photo attachment and manual weekly facts intake are pending the reporting-workflow stage. Existing Friday 8 AM Eastern reminders remain active.
