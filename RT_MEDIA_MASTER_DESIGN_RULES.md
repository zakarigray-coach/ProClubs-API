# RT Football Media — Master Locked Design Rules

These rules are the permanent production design contract for RT Football Media graphics.

## 1. Locked club template mapping

### Birmingham City
- Newspaper template: the approved Birmingham City newspaper reference supplied by the owner (reference image 1).
- Player Spotlight template: the approved Birmingham City spotlight reference supplied by the owner (reference image 2).
- Visual identity: Birmingham City blue, white, and black.
- Use the real Birmingham City crest from project assets.
- Competition wording: Masters Premier League — League 1 / MPL League 1.

### CrownFC
- Player Spotlight template: the approved CrownFC spotlight reference supplied by the owner (reference image 3).
- Newspaper template: the approved CrownFC newspaper reference supplied by the owner (reference image 4).
- Visual identity: black/deep navy, Carolina/cyan blue, royal blue, silver, and white. Never use gold.
- Use the real CrownFC crest from project assets.
- Competition wording: Major League Pro Clubs / MLPC.

## 2. Newspaper template — locked behavior

Every newspaper must retain the approved RT Football Media front-page newspaper theme for that club. Layout details may adapt slightly to content, but the design family, hierarchy, club identity, masthead treatment, main headline area, hero image area, right-side sidebar, and bottom feature strip must remain recognizable as the approved reference.

### Required structure
- RT Football Media masthead.
- Correct club crest and league branding.
- Main headline.
- Main subheadline.
- Short source line.
- Main hero image.
- Right sidebar headed in the approved “Inside Today” style.
- Bottom club feature/slogan strip.

### Sidebar content — hard lock
The sidebar contains exactly four key-story slots.

1. Key Story 1 — one unique topic/angle.
2. Key Story 2 — a different unique topic/angle.
3. Key Story 3 — a third different unique topic/angle.
4. Key Story 4 — PLAYER QUOTE ONLY.

Key Story 4 must always be the supplied player quote when a real quote exists. It must not be replaced by another recap topic, league note, generic quick hit, or duplicate summary.

### No-repeat rule
- Key Stories 1–3 must not repeat the same subject, fact, or angle.
- Key Story 4 must not duplicate any of Key Stories 1–3.
- If generated sidebar copy overlaps, rewrite it before rendering.
- Never repeat the lead headline summary in the sidebar.

### Length and fit
- Key Stories 1–3: concise, normally 2–3 visual lines each.
- Key Story 4: player quote only, normally no more than 2 visual lines.
- Use complete thoughts and complete sentences where applicable.
- No ellipses used to hide overflow.
- No mid-sentence clipping.
- If copy does not fit, shorten/rewrite the copy; do not shrink into unreadable text.

## 3. Player Spotlight — locked behavior

Player Spotlight is a separate premium magazine/editorial product and must never be rendered as a newspaper page.

### Required visual treatment
- RT Football Media Player Spotlight branding.
- Correct club name, crest, competition, and season.
- One dominant featured-player portrait.
- Supporting secondary player imagery/panels as appropriate.
- Position.
- Squad number.
- Player name/gamertag.
- Strong quote callout.
- Short interview/feature excerpts.
- Footer identifying club, competition, and season.

### Variation
The spotlight may shift secondary image placement, supporting panel arrangement, or hero balance to avoid identical posts, but it must remain clearly within the approved club-specific spotlight design family.

## 4. Multiple-player signing template — separate locked product

The multiple-signing graphic is its own signing-announcement template. It must not look like the newspaper or Player Spotlight template.

### Core rules
- Supports 2–5 players.
- One club per graphic.
- One unified coordinated composition — never separate mini-posters pasted together.
- Correct club crest and competition logo/identifier.
- RT Football Media branding.
- Clear player names and positions/numbers.
- Player order must match management’s selected left-to-right order.
- Preserve each supplied player’s recognizable face, hair, facial hair, skin tone, build, accessories, and visible sleeve style.
- Never invent a face when no identity photo is supplied; use back-facing or face-obscured treatment for that slot instead.
- Optional marquee player may receive only slight center/foreground prominence and only when management explicitly designates that player.
- Never auto-select a marquee player.

### Birmingham City group signing
- Birmingham City blue/white/black palette.
- Real Birmingham City crest.
- MPL / Masters Premier League — League 1 identity.
- Unified high-energy Birmingham signing-class treatment.

### CrownFC group signing
- Black/deep navy/cyan/Carolina blue/white/silver palette.
- Real CrownFC crest.
- MLPC identity.
- Never use gold.
- Unified high-energy CrownFC signing-class treatment.

## 5. Accuracy and separation rules

- Never mix Birmingham City and CrownFC branding in one club graphic.
- Never invent or redraw a club crest when the real project asset exists.
- Newspaper, Player Spotlight, and Multiple Signing are three separate products with separate layout systems.
- Player photos, names, squad numbers, positions, quotes, headlines, and article copy are dynamic.
- Club identity, template family, visual hierarchy, crest usage, league identity, and RT Football Media branding are locked.

## 6. Production validation before owner preview

Before a preview is shown to the owner, verify:
- correct club template selected;
- correct crest and league identity;
- all names/numbers/positions mapped to the correct player;
- no mixed club branding;
- no clipped or unreadable text;
- no repeated newspaper sidebar topics;
- newspaper Key Story 4 is the real player quote when one exists;
- group-signing left-to-right player mapping is correct;
- no player face is invented when no identity reference was supplied.

These rules are production locks and should be treated as the source of truth for RT Football Media visual output.
